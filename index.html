Before you start

Five things to have ready

  1. Your sign-in. Your instructor gives you an IAM user name and password for the shared training account.
  2. Region: US West (Oregon), us-west-2. Check the Region selector every time you open a new service.
  3. Your MY_ID. Your seat number plus two random characters, lowercase, for example p07x42. Use the same one in every mission. and every page shows your real resource names.
  4. Shared-account manners. Never edit or delete anything that does not contain your MY_ID. Resources that start with orbit-academy- are shared and read-only.
  5. A browser. Current Chrome, Edge or Firefox. No installs; AWS CloudShell covers the few terminal steps.
